About Tesouro

Professional services with a security mindset.

Tesouro Security is a UK business providing private security activities, security systems services, management consultancy and specialist disinfecting and exterminating services.

Our approach

Commercially aware. Operationally focused.

Our role is to help clients maintain safe, controlled and professionally managed environments. We focus on clear requirements, practical risk management, responsible conduct and dependable communication.

Whether the requirement involves people, technology, management support or environmental services, we aim to provide a proportionate solution with clear accountability.

Principles

The standards behind our work.

Integrity

We expect professional, lawful and ethical conduct throughout our operations and business relationships.

Accountability

Responsibilities, escalation routes and service expectations should be understood from the outset.

Proportionality

Security and operational controls should reflect the actual risks and requirements of the environment.

Continuous improvement

We review delivery and feedback to identify practical opportunities to strengthen performance.
